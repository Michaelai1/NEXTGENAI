Findings & corrections

The loop that is your actual job.

Spotting the problem is the easy part. Proving it got fixed is the part that lives in email threads and gets lost. This closes it.

STEP 01

Logged on site

Your inspector records the finding where they are standing, with a photo, tied to the client and the job site. No writing it up back at the office three hours later.

STEP 02

Assigned with a date

It goes to the right person at the client with a correction due date. It sits open, and visible, until somebody does something about it.

STEP 03

Closed with evidence

Corrected, photographed, and closed out against the original finding. Repeat findings across a client surface on their own — that is your next training session.
