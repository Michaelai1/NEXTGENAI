How it works

Three steps.

No app to download. No training required. A crew either scans the code on the machine or replies to the text that just came in — the office and shop see it immediately.

STEP 01

Scan a code, or reply to a text

Either entry point works from any smartphone with no app and no login. Scanning opens a form already loaded with the asset ID. Replying to the scheduled text opens the form already tied to that crew and that day.

STEP 02

Submit it with photos

Pick the type — inspection, service, transport, daily log, JHA, hot work permit, toolbox talk. Add required photos and a description. Hit submit. Done in under a minute.

STEP 03

One queue, tracked status

Everything lands in a single dashboard for the office and shop. Asset ID, crew, job site, location, photos, and status tracking all in one place. Can connect to your existing software.
